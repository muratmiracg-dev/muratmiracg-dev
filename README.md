I am a **Statistics student at Süleyman Demirel University** building my career around **banking analytics, risk analytics, insurance analytics, and business intelligence**.

My work focuses on turning financial and operational information into **risk indicators, explainable models, executive dashboards, scoring systems, scenario analyses, and practical decision-support tools**. My strongest portfolio areas are **Credit Risk, AML/KYC & Financial Crime, Treasury & ALM, IRRBB, Liquidity Risk, SME Relationship Management, and Banking Sales Intelligence**.

I also bring hands-on commercial experience from founding and operating an e-commerce business, which helps me connect technical work with real questions around **profitability, pricing, growth, customer experience, inventory, marketing efficiency, and operational constraints**.

> **Portfolio transparency:** Unless a repository explicitly states otherwise, portfolio datasets and business outputs are controlled or synthetic. They are designed to demonstrate analytical methodology, engineering quality, validation, business interpretation, and decision-support design rather than represent real customer or institutional data.
