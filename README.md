### 🔬 Research: Parametric insurance for solar power plants

**TÜBİTAK 2209-A undergraduate research project (in progress).** Compares a traditional irradiance-based parametric insurance index with a machine-learning production index for solar power plants in Konya, Niğde, Karaman and Sivas, and measures how much **basis risk**, the gap between index payouts and actual production losses, each design leaves.

- Hourly plant-level generation from the EPİAŞ Transparency Platform; NASA POWER and ERA5-Land meteorology.
- OLS, Ridge and Random Forest production models; basis risk scored with an asymmetric error that penalises under-payment more than over-payment; designs compared at equal premium budget.
- Sensitivity to the meteorological data source, the training period and the payout trigger.
